# Hi, I'm Nachiket

I'm an MBA student at Harvard Business School (Class of 2028). Before HBS I spent four years at ITC, one of India's largest consumer goods companies, running plant operations and then leading digital transformation across 11 plants. I also co-founded Eye in the Sky, a drone computer-vision startup, while at IIT Madras.

I build small, practical tools with AI, mostly to make my own work and the work of people around me easier.
